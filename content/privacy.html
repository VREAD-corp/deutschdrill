Privacy notice

Short version: no cookies, no account, no advertising trackers. Your texts and voice recordings stay on your device. We store your e-mail only if you join the early-access list.

1. Your texts and recordings: not collected

  • The self-check runs in your browser. What you type is saved only in your browser's local storage, so a draft survives a reload. “Delete text” removes it; clearing your browser data does too.
  • Speaking recordings use your browser's microphone (MediaRecorder). The audio stays in the open tab's memory. It is never uploaded. Closing the tab or pressing “Delete recording” removes it. If you press “Save the recording”, the file goes to your own device only.

2. Anonymous counts

When you open a page or press certain buttons, your browser sends us: the page path, the name of the action (for example “check_done”), the domain of the site that sent you here, utm campaign tags if the link had them, whether the screen is narrower than 768 pixels, and on the early-access page which test price and currency were shown. No cookie, no identifier, no user agent; the request is sent without credentials, and our script (Google Apps Script) does not receive your IP address. We store daily totals, not individual visits. If your browser sends “Do Not Track” or “Global Privacy Control”, nothing is sent. Search-engine crawlers and automated browsers, recognised inside your browser without their signature being sent, are counted separately, and a page your browser preloads is counted only if you open it. The test price shown to you is remembered in your browser's session storage until you close the tab, so it does not change while you browse.

Because these counts cannot identify you, we consider them not personal data; we describe them so you can judge for yourself.

3. The early-access list

  • What: your e-mail, the options you ticked (offer, plan, level, exam, exam month), the test price and currency shown, the campaign tag of your visit and the date.
  • Why: to e-mail you once when the paid options open, and to count how many people want them at which price.
  • Legal basis: your consent (DPDP Act section 6; GDPR article 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time by e-mail; withdrawing is as easy as giving it.
  • Where: in a Google Sheet operated by READ FRANCE SAS. Google acts as our processor and may store data outside India and the EU; transfers to the US rely on Google's standard contractual clauses and the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.
  • How long: until the opening e-mail is sent and at most 12 months from your sign-up, then deleted. Earlier if you ask.
  • Not done: no sale, no sharing with institutes or agents, no marketing beyond the one opening e-mail, no automated decisions about you.
  • Age: the list is for people aged 18 or over, as the DPDP Act requires verifiable parental consent for children's data.

4. Hosting

The pages are hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any web host, GitHub may process technical data such as IP addresses for security, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We do not receive those logs.

5. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can ask for a summary of your data and how it is processed, for correction, completion or erasure, and you can nominate someone to exercise your rights. Under the GDPR you also have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. Write to {{contact}}; we answer within 30 days. If you are not satisfied you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India or, in the EU, to the French authority CNIL or your local authority.
