What we plan to offer

Expert-style feedback on your writing within 24 h

You submit a text written in one of our tasks. You get back: the criteria the exam uses (task completion, coherence, vocabulary, structures) each rated with a short reason, your text with corrections marked, and three things to fix first.

How it would be made: by an AI model (a large language model) instructed with the published exam criteria, then checked by our own rules. No human examiner reads it, and it is not an exam score. “Expert-style” means it follows the examiners' criteria, nothing more.

Mock speaking exam

The full A2 or B1 speaking module in your browser with exam timing: preparation, planning task with an AI partner, presentation, follow-up questions. Afterwards you get a transcript of what you said with comments on the same criteria.

How it would be made: your recording would have to be sent to a speech-to-text service and an AI model. We would ask for your consent each time, delete the audio after processing, and say which providers we use before you start. You would always know you are talking to an AI.

Neither option exists yet, so there is no real sample of it to show. The closest real thing is the free self-check, which already flags word count, greeting, word order and case errors: try it on an A1 task.
