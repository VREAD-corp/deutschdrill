## Hard rules for every channel
1. Say who we are: "DeutschDrill, a free practice site by READ FRANCE SAS (Paris)". Octave never poses as a learner, teacher or "student who passed".
2. No fake reviews, no sock-puppet answers, no "I used this and passed" claims. No pass-rate or score claims.
3. Never use Goethe or telc logos; say "Goethe/telc exam format", never "official" or "partner".
4. Post only where the group rules allow resources or self-promotion; read the rules first, one post per group, answer questions in the thread.
5. Lead with a free, specific item (a task + self-check link, the INR fee table, the passport-only ID rule), not with the paid early-access page.
6. UTM on every link: `?utm_source=<channel>&utm_medium=<social|community|email|video>&utm_campaign=launch-oct26`.
7. No ad spend. No buying followers or group access. No scraping of member lists or phone numbers.
